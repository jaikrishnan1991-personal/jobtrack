LinkedIn sync (Apify)

Runs the queries × locations in config.json → linkedin through a no-login Apify actor, enriches each posting heuristically and scores it here. Unmet mandatories are keyword-matched — verify before applying. Takes 3–10 minutes.

Recruiter emails

        

        
      

      
Scans job descriptions already in the tracker for a contact address — free, no Apify call.
        Most boards deliberately hide recruiter addresses behind their own apply flow, so expect a low hit rate;
        where nothing is found you can paste an address into the email composer yourself.
